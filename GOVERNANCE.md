# Governance

## Maintainer organization
BAX Print Suite is maintained by **BAX Forward** (GitHub org `BAX-Forward`). BAX Forward sets the roadmap, holds the repository, and has final decision authority while the project is young.

## Roles
- **Contributors:** anyone who submits issues, docs, code, or reviews.
- **Committers:** trusted contributors with triage rights and review responsibility for specific areas (listed in `CODEOWNERS`).
- **Maintainers:** merge rights, release management, and ADR approval.

## Becoming a committer or maintainer
- **Committer:** a track record of quality contributions (typically 5+ merged non-trivial PRs or sustained review work) and nomination by a maintainer, with no maintainer objection within 7 days.
- **Maintainer:** sustained committer activity for 6+ months and nomination by a maintainer, approved by a majority of maintainers, including BAX Forward.
- Inactive roles (12 months) may move to emeritus.

## Decisions
- Day-to-day: lazy consensus on PRs.
- Architecture and interface changes: an ADR under `docs/adr/`, open for comment for at least 5 business days, approved by maintainers.
- Disputes: maintainers vote by simple majority. BAX Forward breaks ties.

## Evolution
As the community grows, we intend to move to a steering committee that includes non-BAX Forward maintainers. Changes to this document need an ADR.
